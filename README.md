# Cryptikware Communications Inc.

AI governance, safety-critical systems design, and responsible AI implementation
for healthcare, aviation, emergency response, and regulated industries.

## What we work on

- **AI governance** — frameworks and review practices for deploying AI systems
  in environments with regulatory and safety obligations.
- **Safety-critical systems design** — engineering approaches for domains where
  failure modes carry real-world consequences.
- **Responsible AI implementation** — applied work bridging model capabilities
  and the operational, ethical, and compliance constraints of regulated sectors.

## Domains

Healthcare · Aviation · Emergency response · Other regulated industries.
