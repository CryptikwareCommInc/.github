# Security Policy

Cryptikware Communications Inc. takes the security of its software and systems
seriously. This policy explains how to report a vulnerability and what to
expect after you do.

## Reporting a Vulnerability

Please report security vulnerabilities by email to **info@cryptikware.com**.

Do **not** open a public issue, pull request, or discussion for a security
concern, and do not include exploit details or sensitive information in any
public channel.

When reporting, please include where relevant:

- The repository or component affected.
- A description of the issue and its potential impact.
- Steps to reproduce, or a proof of concept, if available.
- Any suggested remediation.

## Coordinated Disclosure

We follow a coordinated disclosure approach:

- We ask that you give us a reasonable opportunity to investigate and remediate
  before any public disclosure.
- We will not pursue or support legal action against researchers who report in
  good faith and act in accordance with this policy.
- Please make a good-faith effort to avoid privacy violations, data
  destruction, and service disruption while investigating.

## What to Expect

- **Acknowledgement:** We aim to acknowledge receipt of your report within a
  few business days.
- **Updates:** We will keep you informed of progress as we assess and address
  the report.
- **Resolution:** Once resolved, we will coordinate with you on any disclosure
  timing.

Timelines are targets, not guarantees, and may vary with the severity and
complexity of the issue.

## Scope

This policy applies to repositories and software published under the
Cryptikware Communications Inc. organization. Third-party dependencies and
services are governed by their respective providers' security policies.
